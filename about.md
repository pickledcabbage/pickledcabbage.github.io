I am Dima, a software engineer with many years of experience across distributed systems, machine learning, autonomous vehicles, and low-level programming.

The goal of this blog is two-fold. First I have accumulated over the years a lot of miscellaneous links, blog posts, research papers, guides, ebooks and all sorts of other materials during my study of computer science, so I want to have a singular place to store them instead of scattering them across Chrome tabs, Obsidian pages, work laptops, etc. Secondly I want to share my insights and technical knowledge of the software industry as well improve my technical writing skills. From my intial estimate, there will be a lot more of the former than latter.

Hopefully you find some interesting tidbits here and there in the blog.
